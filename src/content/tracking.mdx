I like having access to basic analytics about how, where, and when visitors interact with my blog. I do not, however, enjoy identifying information being collected about me for no reason.

Likewise, I have no reason to collect identifying information about you—not even the "not-quite-identifying" stuff someone could corroborate with a big database to [reidentify](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Data_re-identification) you later on. This site doesn't need to enable me nor an analytics platform access to that information. Even with tweaked settings and the like, I do not have sufficient trust that analytics platforms aimed for use on the corporate internet will respect those preferences at all times. I, myself, block those trackers on all my devices.

At present, I have sufficient trust in the [GoatCounter](https://www.goatcounter.com/) analytics platform. Its [source](https://github.com/arp242/goatcounter) is [free](https://www.gnu.org/philosophy/free-sw.en.html) and open to the public, it's [made for small operations](https://www.goatcounter.com/why), and it's designed to track so little that it [likely doesn't necessitate a privacy notice](https://www.goatcounter.com/help/gdpr) for a site to remain [GDPR](https://commission.europa.eu/law/law-topic/data-protection_en)-compliant.

This site uses GoatCounter to track pageviews, country and region, screen size, browser and system name ([user agent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/User_agent)), unique visits ([identified on the server by user agent and IP address](https://www.goatcounter.com/help/sessions#technical-details-13544)—not even by a cookie), and the site that referred a visit (["referer"](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/HTTP_referer)). A small script supplied by GoatCounter runs in your browser to send this information to GoatCounter's servers, if your browser supports it; otherwise, your browser gets a tiny image whose loading sends over some of this information.

You can view all the data collected by GoatCounter—the same data I see—[online](https://stats.dandelion.computer). At your own privacy discretion, feel free to block or unblock GoatCounter's domains: `gc.zgo.at` and `goatcounter.com`.

If you have concerns about how this site collects and uses information about visitors, please [contact me](/contact.pdf).
